Two particles in a 2D universe. Particle 1 (the source) is held fixed at the origin; particle 2 (the probe) moves. Your task: discover the law of motion governing particle 2. Variables: r = distance between the two particles [0.3, 10]; p1 = scalar property of the source particle [0.1, 10]; p2 = scalar property of the probe particle [0.1, 10]; t = time since the start of the experiment [0, 5]. Each experiment sets p1 and p2; the probe's initial position and velocity are fixed and identical for every experiment, and the experiment returns the probe's position at the measurement times t = 0.5, 1.0, ..., 5.0. The laws of physics in this universe may differ from the real world.
